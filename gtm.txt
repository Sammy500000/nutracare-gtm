                                                        GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY

                                                    CORE THESIS
To cater to the nutrition gap among pregnant & lactating women and infant & young

                                                       children.

                                           THESIS METHODOLOGY

Leveraging CSR-funded free distribution through NGO networks to establish trust across rural India
and becoming a trusted choice in urban households through online and offline channels, our mission
is to deliver the right nutrition at the right time while creating measurable social impact at scale.
